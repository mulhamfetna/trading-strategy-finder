🕳️ Three stories from the deep (without which errors would have been published)

Control contamination — programme-wide

The V3 falsifier failed 4/4: "no-release-day" controls showed a phantom jump of 2.3–2.5×. Cause: "no release" meant "no release from our list" — and 8:30 ET carries Jobless Claims, PPI, GDP on "quiet" days. Fix: controls cleaned against the full 39k-event calendar, then a quiet-noon falsifier (0.89–0.94× = the pipeline manufactures nothing).

The units lie in 1-second files

"Median time-to-TP 239 seconds" appeared — before the release itself! It was 239 bars: 1-second files carry bars only for traded seconds (NQ: 203 of 300 pre-release seconds; RTY: only 116). Caught before any publication; the rule: a bar offset is never a duration.

The CL inversion — the falsifier at work

On crude oil: the no-news control beats the release itself (+$29 vs −$2 for the long) — the bracket's profit source there exists everywhere, so nothing is attributable to news ⇐ CL's verdict is VOID per the pre-registration, not "negative".

The frame bridge (V1)

The 1-second pipeline reproduces M1's cell computed on 1-minute bars: +$80.80 vs +$84.24 on exactly the same 574 events — two frames, two codebases, one trade.

OWNER DECISIONS The two decisions that gate deployment — explained in levels ▶

The research is finished; what remains is not an experiment but two decisions only the owner can make. Each is explained here at four depths — from a single sentence down to the finest risk numbers.

Decision 1 — the execution engine: a sizing/multi-leg layer + a schedule-driven release executor
L0The one sentence Without this decision the programme's result stays knowledge, not P&L: the current system cannot even open the confirmed trade.
L1Why? Two structural blockers (1) The engine's profit equation pnl = pnl_points × pv has no quantity term — always one contract, no way to express size or two legs. (2) The engine is candle-and-indicator based, while the confirmed trade lives at 1-second resolution as a bracket (timed entry, stop, take-profit, timed exit) and needs no indicator at all.
L2Exactly what is needed (a) A sizing/multi-leg layer in the P&L accounting. (b) A small standalone release executor: CPI/NFP/FOMC timestamps are known in advance (≈52 per year) — an executor that wakes five minutes before each, places the bracket, and sleeps. It does not need the strategy engine at all, so the change is far smaller than an engine refactor.
L3What else this decision unlocks + the economics The same change unlocks every "magnitude without direction" finding back to WS-EARN (the 4.98× earnings-second volatility) that stayed unusable for the same reason. At one contract: NQ ≈ +$6,900/yr net stressed + RTY ≈ +$1,800 — modest on its own, but linear in quantity: this decision is what turns it from a research number into a scalable sleeve. Risk budgeting must count the losing leg as potentially 2R, not 1R (worst measured loss: −$995 on a nominal ≈$476 stop — gap-through-stop slippage in the sweep second).
Decision 2 — the regime monitor: the operating condition before any live deployment
L0The one sentence The premium funding this trade is a child of its era — no deployment without an alarm that halts it automatically if the era dies.
L1Why? The era evidence The winning cell's per-event profit on NQ: 2016–19: +$59 · 2020–21: +$24 · 2022+: +$292. The sign is positive in all three eras (which is what the halves rule certified), but the magnitude lives in the current inflation era — and the sample cannot separate "a permanent premium amplified by macro uncertainty" from "a temporary regime that will fade". Honesty demands the alarm be built before going live, not after.
L2The proposed operating rule (to be pinned before launch) (a) A rolling mean over the last 24 CPI events of the trade's P&L: if it drops below zero ⇐ immediate stand-down (24 events = two years of CPI — the same horizon as the power model's window). (b) Re-run the claims ledger annually (optimize/verify/run.py): any drift between published numbers and their files lights up automatically. (c) Review the rule itself at the first alarm — halting is one decision, restarting is another.
L3The risk numbers to decide against (1) n = 116 CPI events carry the trade's economics — a two-year CPI regime change flips it. (2) The monitor's own rolling window lags up to two years behind a fast-dying regime (the same lesson M2 taught). (3) Fills inside the release second are assumed at line-or-open on 1-second bars; the stressed scenario carries 4 ticks and the worst measured slippage is 2.1× the stop — anything beyond that needs live tick data we do not have (a declared blind spot in the claims). (4) The distribution's nature: most weeks lose small, and three or four CPI seconds make the year — without this understanding, live performance will feel like a betrayal of the backtest while matching it exactly.
