EV/Trade stands for Expected Value per Trade. It is a statistical calculation that tells you the average amount of money you can expect to win or lose on every single trade over the long run, based on your historical win rate and Profit/Loss (P/L) ratio.
## How it Works
Expected Value combines your probability of winning with the size of your wins and losses to show if your trading system has a "mathematical edge."

* Formula: $\text{EV/Trade} = (\text{Win Probability} \times \text{Average Gain}) - (\text{Loss Probability} \times \text{Average Loss})$
* Example: Suppose your strategy has a 40% win rate (0.40) and a 60% loss rate (0.60). Your average winning trade (TP) is $\$300$ and your average losing trade (SL) is $\$100$.
$$\text{EV/Trade} = (0.40 \times \$300) - (0.60 \times \$100) = \$120 - \$60 = +\$60$$ This means that despite losing more often than you win, you can expect to make an average of $60 per trade over hundreds of trades.

## Interpreting the Outcome

* Positive EV (+$): Your system has a positive edge. If you execute the strategy consistently and manage your risk, you will mathematically make money over time.
* Negative EV (-$): Your system has a negative edge (like casino games). No matter how good your psychology is, you will mathematically lose money in the long run.

## The Complete Performance Loop
EV/Trade acts as the ultimate summary metric that ties all your previously discussed trading metrics together:

   1. You use indicators like the RSI to find entry points.
   2. You set your Stop-Loss (SL) and Take-Profit (TP) orders to establish your P/L Ratio.
   3. Your historical success with those setups determines your win rate, resulting in your EV/Trade.
   4. If your EV/Trade is highly positive, your account balance grows smoothly, which naturally lowers your Max Drawdown and boosts your Sharpe Ratio.
