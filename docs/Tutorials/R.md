In TradingView, the letter "R" usually refers to Risk (1R) or a Risk-to-Reward ratio (R:R), which is tracked natively through the platform's drawing toolbar. However, depending on context, it can also refer to specific technical indicators or chart formats. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7] 
------------------------------
## 1. Risk and Risk-to-Reward (R Multiple)
The most common use of "R" is as a unit of measurement for financial risk. [2, 8] 

* 1R Definition: The absolute dollar amount you stand to lose on a trade if the market hits your stop loss.
* Example: If your account has $10,000 and you risk 1% per trade, 1R = $100. [2, 9] 
* R Multiples: Your target returns are scaled using your risk.
* If your trade hits a take-profit level that yields $300 while risking $100, that is a +3R trade. [2, 8, 9] 
* TradingView Tool: When you select the Long Position or Short Position drawing tool on the left-hand toolbar, TradingView automatically plots a dual-colored box on your chart. The center displays your exact Risk/Reward Ratio (e.g., 2.5 means you win 2.5R for every 1R you risk). [4, 5, 6] 

------------------------------
## 2. Williams %R (%R)
If you see "R" inside the indicators menu, it most likely refers to the Williams %R momentum oscillator. [1] 

* What it does: It oscillates between 0 and -100 to show where a security's current closing price sits relative to its highest high over a given period (typically 14 days). [1, 10] 
* How to read it: Readings between 0 and -20 indicate the market is overbought, while readings between -80 and -100 suggest it is oversold. [1, 10] 

------------------------------
## 3. Range Bars (R Charting Type)
If "R" is written next to a timeframe number in the top menu bar (e.g., 10R), it means you are looking at a Range Bar chart. [7] 

* What it does: Unlike traditional charts that print a new candle after a set amount of time (like 5 minutes), Range bars ignore time entirely. [7, 11, 12] 
* How to read it: A new bar forms only when the price moves by a specific number of minimum price increments (ticks). 10R means a new candle prints every time the price spans a 10-tick range. [7] 

------------------------------
