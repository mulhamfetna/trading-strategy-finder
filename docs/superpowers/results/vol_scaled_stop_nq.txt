  The point of the sigma-stop is CONSISTENCY: a constant stop-out probability across regimes,
  vs the fixed stop whose stop% swings by regime (tight & often-hit when loud, ~never-hit when

  delivers consistent risk. P/L may be SIMILAR-or-worse in the BACKTEST (D1: the backtest caps

  cannot show. Do NOT adopt on backtest P/L alone; adopt for risk consistency + live gap safety.
