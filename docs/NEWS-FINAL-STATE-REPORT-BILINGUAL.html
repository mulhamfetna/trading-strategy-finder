L0 The whole story in one card · القصة كلها في بطاقة

After ~1,300 measured series×instrument cells across five workstreams, the US economic calendar contains, at our trade shape and our costs, exactly one harvestable thing: the announcement premium of the CPI print on stock-index futures. It is deployed on all four index legs. Everything else is a powered null, an unverdictable minute, the fee schedule made visible — or, in one famous case, a confirmed negative.

L1 What became profitable, what didn't — and WHY · ما ربح وما لم يربح ولماذا
family · العائلةverdictthe why · السبب
CPI on stock indexes (NQ +$309 · ES +$151 · YM +$108 · RTY +$78 net/ev) PROFITABLE — all DEPLOYED The one moment the market systematically pays to hold risk through: macro uncertainty resolves at 08:30 and equity indexes reprice UP through it on average (the announcement-premium mechanism). Ordered by index beta; convex (small stop, big target) so the +4R tail pays for the losing median.
NFP / FOMC (inside the NQ+RTY pooled trade)carried, not standalone Alone they never confirm anywhere (net ≈ 0 to negative); the pooled {CPI,NFP,FOMC} trade was confirmed as a SET on NQ/RTY and stays deployed as verified. On new instruments only CPI shipped.
Retail Sales (gross-negative on 7 instruments)ANTI-PREMIUM — do-not-ride The only release where riding long systematically LOSES on gross across the whole market. Mechanism unproven (RQ-2 will ask if the short side pays); status: confirmed exclusion.
The other ~90 calendar moments (Jobless Claims, PPI, ISM, GDP, JOLTs, ADP, Michigan, Minutes, auctions, speeches…)POWERED NULLS / UNDERPOWERED They genuinely move price (jump gates 1.6–8×) yet pay nothing — POWER ≠ PREMIUM. On NQ every one of them could have shown a CPI-sized premium and didn't.
Energy inventories (EIA/API on CL; EIA-natgas on NG)definitive NO The most violent scheduled minutes in their tapes (5–8.5×) gross ≈ $0 at n=551/433/552 — volatility without direction, measured with the heaviest power in the programme.
Metals at CPI (HG gross +$80, SI +$48/ev)cost-drowned Positive gross, eaten by $52.50/$102.50 cost lines. Not deployable at current cost structure; queued (RQ-5: micro contracts / measured spreads).
370 VOID-TIMESTAMP cells (Redbook, MBA, NFIB, most auction stamps, half the Fed speeches…)unverdictable Their calendar minute does not move that tape (jump ≤1.2×) — the timestamp can't be trusted, so no premium verdict is claimable either way. Recorded, not guessed.
القراءة: الرابح الوحيد هو علاوة CPI على المؤشرات (مرتّبة ببيتا المؤشر: ناسداك ثم إس&بي ثم داو ثم راسل). NFP وFOMC «محمولان» داخل صفقة NQ/RTY المجمّعة المؤكدة ولا يؤكَّدان منفردين. التجزئة عكسية مؤكدة على سبع أدوات. وبقية التقويم: إما أصفار مُقوّاة رغم عنف الدقيقة (القوة ≠ العائد)، أو مخزونات طاقة تتحرك بعنف وتدفع صفرًا، أو معادن موجبة الإجمالي تأكلها التكاليف، أو 370 خلية بطوابع زمنية لا تحرك السعر أصلًا فلا حكم عليها.
L2 The deployed structure in detail + the profit tables · البنية المنشورة والأرباح

The one trade, four legs · الصفقة الواحدة بأربعة أرجل

Every leg runs the SAME frozen bracket: LONG at the close of the 1-second bar at release−300 s · stop 0.10 % (worse-of line/open) · take-profit 0.40 % (better-of, resting) · both-in-one-bar ⇒ STOP · timed exit at release+900 s. Decisions are mechanical: the schedule says when, the executor does the rest.
