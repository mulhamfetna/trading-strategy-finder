Put your market CSVs here (or use --data <dir>). Naming:
  <INST>_<tf>.csv   e.g. GC_4h.csv   (Date,Open,High,Low,Close)
  <INST>_1m.csv     e.g. GC_1m.csv   (Date,Open,High,Low,Close)
  <INST>_box.csv    e.g. GC_box.csv  (Date,<weekly/monthly box level columns>)
INST in {NQ,ES,GC,SI,RTY,YM}. Non-NQ box = the -1-workday-shifted box.
